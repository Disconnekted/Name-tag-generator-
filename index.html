Name Tag Generator

by Joseph McLaren

1. Your details

🔒 Shared computer: your classes are saved under your name. Click Switch at the top when you're done. You're also signed out after 30 minutes without use.

Tag style

2. Add students

Students come straight from the TOPIA attendance page, using the Copy students button.

  1. One time only: show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B), then drag this button onto it: Copy students
  2. On the TOPIA Attendance page (the list of your classes for a day), click Copy students in your bookmarks bar. It opens each class's list by itself and closes it again with Cancel. It never presses Save.
  3. When it's finished, click Copy for name tags in the red box.
  4. Come back here, click the box below and press Ctrl+V. Every class and student comes in at once, with the days filled in.
  5. Classes that meet on other days? Change the date on the TOPIA page and repeat. New classes are added, and days like Mon / Wed build up automatically.

3. Check each class

Tick the students who need a new tag (a new student, or a lost tag). Only those tags print, packed together on as few pages as possible. Need two of someone? Change ×1 to ×2 next to their name. Untick everyone

Pick the subject and days (add a class time if you like, it prints before the days), and untick classes you don't need. Use ×2 or ×3 next to a class to print extra copies of each tag. Names come from TOPIA. A student missing? Type their name in the box under the class and click Add.
